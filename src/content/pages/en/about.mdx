Industrial Wireless I/O is an English-language reference hub for industrial wireless I/O and industrial wireless connectivity: wireless I/O modules and gateways, wireless protocols (WirelessHART, ISA100.11a, LoRaWAN, IO-Link Wireless, NB-IoT/LTE-M and private 5G), and link, antenna and site engineering for factory automation, process industry and utility telemetry.

This site is the wireless-connectivity layer of a three-site industrial reference cluster. iiotdevices.com covers IIoT devices and architecture; sensorcatalog.com covers sensor specifications and selection. This site covers how those devices and sensors connect wirelessly.

## How to use this site

- **Products** — the [product database](/product-families/) holds wireless I/O modules, gateways, adapters and routers as structured records, every specification transcribed from the manufacturer's datasheet or official documentation.
- **Protocols** — the [protocol reference](/protocols/) covers the wireless standards themselves: what each one is, where it fits, and how the alternatives compare, with parameters cited to the standards body or the manufacturer.
- **Tools** — the [engineering tools](/tools/) run the link calculations in the browser: a link budget calculator and a Fresnel zone calculator, with the formulas and their sources stated on the page.
- **Guides** — the reference library ([network paths](/network-paths/), [hardware](/hardware/), [reliability](/reliability/)) covers backhaul design, antennas, power, enclosures and the operating discipline that keeps unattended sites trustworthy.
- **Applications** — the [application notes](/applications/) work through site types — pump stations, tank farms, substations, wellheads — and the wireless architecture each one actually needs.

The portal sections on the home page — industry news, market coverage, the vendor ecosystem, technical depth, events and resources — track what is changing across those five reference areas.

## Sourcing policy

Product and protocol specifications on this site are taken from manufacturer datasheets and standards-body publications; figures that cannot be traced to a primary source are left out. The full policy, including how records are checked and corrected, is in the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/).
